# 🌱 VestWise — Your Wealth, Simplified

> A Goldman Sachs × UT Dallas Hackathon Submission  
> **Track:** Empowering the Everyday Investor

---

## 🧩 The Problem

Most investment platforms are built for experts — cluttered with jargon, complex charts, and intimidating metrics. When markets get volatile, everyday investors are left confused and anxious, often making emotional decisions that hurt their long-term wealth.

**Retail investors don't need more data. They need clarity.**

---

## 💡 Our Solution

VestWise is a jargon-free portfolio management platform designed specifically for first-time and non-expert investors. It helps users:

- Understand where their money is at a glance
- Simulate real-world "What-If" scenarios before making decisions
- Get clear, transparent rebalancing recommendations they can actually act on
- Set and track meaningful financial goals tied to their real life

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 🎯 Guided Onboarding
A 4-step quiz that builds your investor profile without requiring any financial knowledge. No Alpha, Beta, or Sharpe ratios — just simple questions about your goals, timeline, and comfort with risk.

### 📊 Unified Dashboard
One clean view of your entire portfolio — stocks and mutual funds together. Includes a **Portfolio Health Score**, allocation donut chart, growth chart, and plain-English holding descriptions.

### 🔄 Scenario-Driven Rebalancing (Core Feature)
Pick a real-world "What-If" scenario:
- 📉 **Market Crash** — What if markets fall 20%?
- 🌡️ **High Inflation** — What if inflation stays at 8%+ for 2 years?
- 💸 **Need Cash Soon** — What if I need 20% of my funds in 6 months?
- 🎉 **I Got a Raise** — What if I can invest ₹5,000 more per month?

VestWise instantly shows a Before → After allocation, explains *why* in plain English, and displays all associated costs and tax implications upfront.

### 🎯 Goal Tracking
Track progress toward real life goals — buying a home, funding education, building a retirement cushion. Get simple nudges when you're off track.

### 🌱 Life Event Triggers
Tell VestWise about a big life change (new baby, job change, marriage, retirement) and get instant, relevant portfolio guidance.

## 📐 Design Decisions

- **Jargon-free language** throughout — "Safety net" instead of "hedge", "your comfort with drops" instead of "risk tolerance"
- **Portfolio Health Score** gives a single number (0–100) instead of overwhelming metrics
- **Color system** uses green/amber/red intuitively — no financial literacy required to interpret
- **Radical transparency** — every recommendation shows the cost, tax note, and plain-English rationale
